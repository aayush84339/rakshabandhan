# My Didi

Yes, she is my Didi—
forever and ever and ever,
no matter where life takes us,
no matter how the seasons turn.

Wanna know who she is?
She is my everything.
The person I tried to leave a thousand times,
yet somehow, my heart
always found its way back to her.

She is the best sister
a foolish brother like me could ever have,
the kind who may be busy most days,
but whose little moments of care and love
can make the whole world feel softer.

And once, on the night of Techfest Day 3,
she gave me the most beautiful Christmas gift
I have ever received.

Sometimes she is caring,
sometimes she is wonderfully lovely,
and most of the time—
she is busy, busy, busy.
But perhaps that's what makes
the moments I get with her
all the more precious.

If some magical jinn appeared before me
and granted me just one wish,
I wouldn't ask for money,
I wouldn't ask for success,
I wouldn't even ask for a gf. :)

I'd simply say—
“Give me one complete day with my Didi.”
One whole day.
No hurry.
No work.
No distance.
Just her and me and talks,
and enough time to make memories
that could last forever.

She has the most beautiful heart in this world—
a heart that deserves every happiness
this universe could possibly give.

And if someday fate asked me
to choose between my life and hers,
there would be no hesitation,
no second thought.
I would choose her.
Obviously, her.

Because some bonds
aren't measured by blood alone.
Some bonds are written
somewhere deeper—
in the quietest corners of the heart,
where time cannot reach
and distance cannot break them.

It all began
on the 19th of November, 2025.

And if I had the power
to write the ending,
I wouldn't write one at all.

Let it continue
through every tomorrow,
through every lifetime,
through every star that burns in the sky—
at least until the universe itself
runs out of time.

That's my Didi.

And I can bet that
nowhere on this planet,
in this lifetime or any other,
could there ever be
a better Didi for me.
